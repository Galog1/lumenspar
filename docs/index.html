Lumenspar

A Genshin Impact companion for Linux and Windows

Lumenspar is a free, open-source desktop app (MIT license). It has four tools:

Wish Counter

Pity, guarantees and 50/50 results for every banner, read from your in-game wish history in the same way as paimon.moe. Optionally, the history can be backed up to your own Google Drive, so a reinstall or another computer gets it back.

Inventory Scanner

Reads your artifacts, weapons, characters and materials off the screen and writes a .GOOD file for Genshin Optimizer, SEELIE.me and other calculators.

Build Cards

Akasha-style cards for your characters: talents, weapon, artifacts with exact roll counts, final stats and set bonuses, drawn on the character's splash art in the element's colour and saveable as images. Switch the artifact score between CV (crit value) and RV (roll value, counting every substat the build uses). Cards come from your in-game showcase through Enka.Network, or for every character on your account through a HoYoLAB sign-in window that keeps nothing: it is off the record, and Lumenspar never reads your password or cookies. Each build also shows its live Akasha.cv leaderboard rank.

Optimizer & team damage

Finds the best artifacts from your scan for any character, by a damage estimate, stat weights, targets, or an Akasha leaderboard. Build a team of up to four and see each character's exact damage per hit, calculated with Genshin Optimizer's own engine, with every conditional buff as a checkbox. Nothing is typed in: every setting is a choice.

Google Drive backup

Press Sign in with Google on the wish counter page and your wish history is backed up to your own Google Drive, and restored from it on any computer you sign in on. Lumenspar has no server: the backup goes straight from your computer to your Drive. Lumenspar only asks Google for its own hidden app-data folder in your Drive, which cannot see any of your files, and for your email address, to show who is signed in. If you prefer, the app can use a Google Cloud project of your own instead. See the privacy policy.

Web viewer

Once your history is backed up, the Lumenspar viewer shows your pity, 50/50 results and build cards in any browser, phone included. It runs entirely in your browser and reads the backup straight from your Drive.

Download

Releases for Linux (AppImage) and Windows (installer), and the source code, are on GitHub. Lumenspar can update itself from there: a banner appears when a newer release is out, and one click downloads, verifies and installs it.
