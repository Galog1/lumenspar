Your wishes and builds, anywhere

This page shows what the Lumenspar desktop app backs up to your Google Drive: pity and 50/50 results for every banner, and your build cards. It runs entirely in your browser and reads only Lumenspar's own hidden folder in your Drive, which holds none of your other files. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

First sign in with Google in the desktop app (Wish Counter → Google Drive backup) and let it back up. Then sign in here with the same account.

Use your own Google project

If your desktop app uses a Google Cloud project of your own, create a Web application client in that project with https://galog1.github.io as an authorized JavaScript origin, and enter its client ID here. It is remembered in this browser only.
